# Accounts and bios

Set these up at least two weeks before launch day. Tick an account off only when it exists and its bio links to the repository or the landing page.

## Bios

Use these unless a platform needs something shorter or longer. Swap the link for the Polish landing page on Polish channels.

**Short (about 160 characters), EN:**

> Building Ticknest, open-source time tracking for small teams that runs in your own Firebase project. https://ticknest-landing.vercel.app

**Short, PL:**

> Tworzę Ticknest: otwartoźródłowy tracker czasu pracy dla małych zespołów, działający w Twoim własnym projekcie Firebase. https://ticknest-landing.vercel.app/pl/

**Long, EN:**

> Software developer. I'm building Ticknest, an open-source (MIT) time tracker for small teams: timer, timesheet, reports with CSV and PDF export, team roles and budgets. It runs in a Firebase project you own, with no seat fees and no monitoring.
> Repo: https://github.com/kacper-szewczyk/Ticknest · Demo: https://ticknest-pi.vercel.app

**Long, PL:**

> Programista. Tworzę Ticknest, otwartoźródłowy (MIT) tracker czasu pracy dla małych zespołów: timer, karta czasu, raporty z eksportem do CSV i PDF, role w zespole i budżety. Działa w Twoim własnym projekcie Firebase, bez opłat za użytkownika i bez monitorowania.
> Repo: https://github.com/kacper-szewczyk/Ticknest · Demo: https://ticknest-pi.vercel.app

## Channels

### Hacker News

- [ ] Account exists, with the short EN bio in "about".
- [ ] Some comment history before T-0: a few useful comments a week from T-14, on topics you know. No links to Ticknest.
- [ ] Read the [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html).

### Reddit

- [ ] Account in good standing (karma from real comments, not only from your own posts).
- [ ] Short EN bio, with the landing page as the profile link.
- [ ] Read the rules of each subreddit and note the self-promotion limits and the required flair here:
  - [ ] r/selfhosted:
  - [ ] r/opensource:
  - [ ] r/freelance:
  - [ ] r/SideProject:
- [ ] Comment in each subreddit during the warm-up, so the launch post isn't your first activity there.

### Product Hunt

- [ ] Maker profile with the long EN bio and a photo.
- [ ] Upcoming launch page for Ticknest: name, tagline, thumbnail, gallery from `docs/screenshots/en`, the timer GIF.
- [ ] Supporters list (people who said they'd like to hear about the launch). Message them on T-7 and on T-0 with the link to the launch page, asking for feedback, not for votes.

### LinkedIn

- [ ] Profile headline and "About" updated with the long EN bio. Add Ticknest under "Projects" or "Featured" with the landing page link.
- [ ] EN and PL launch posts drafted (see the checklist). Post them as two separate posts, or as one with the PL version below the EN one.

### X, Bluesky and Mastodon

Personal account on each, used for build-in-public posts.

- [ ] X: short EN bio, landing page in the profile link.
- [ ] Bluesky: short EN bio, landing page in the profile link.
- [ ] Mastodon: short EN bio, and the repository and landing page as profile fields. Pick a general or tech instance (for example fosstodon.org, which suits open-source projects).
- [ ] Pinned post on each, once it is live: the launch post.

### Wykop

- [ ] Account with the short PL bio.
- [ ] Some activity in tags such as #programowanie, #opensource and #webdev during the warm-up.
- [ ] Read the rules on adding your own links.

### 4programmers.net

- [ ] Account with the short PL bio and the PL landing page in the signature or profile.
- [ ] Some forum activity during the warm-up.
- [ ] Find the forum section for showing your own projects and read its rules.

### dev.to, Medium or your own blog

- [ ] dev.to account with the long EN bio (the launch article goes here). Medium is optional; if you post there too, set the canonical URL to the dev.to article or your blog.
- [ ] A place for the Polish article: your own blog, or dev.to with a Polish post.

### Indie Hackers

- [ ] Profile with the long EN bio.
- [ ] Ticknest added as a product, with the landing page and the repository.
