# Firestore Rules emulator test matrix

Every row below, the boundary-validation cases, and the callable flows are automated. `npm run test:emulators` runs them against the Auth, Firestore, and Functions emulators:

- [`tests/emulator/firestore-rules.test.ts`](../tests/emulator/firestore-rules.test.ts) has one `matrix(...)` block per row, run as an unauthenticated client, an authenticated user with no membership, a workspace member, a workspace manager, and the workspace owner.
- [`tests/emulator/functions.test.ts`](../tests/emulator/functions.test.ts) calls the workspace, invitation, membership, and role callables with Auth emulator users.
- [`tests/emulator/functions.test.ts`](../tests/emulator/functions.test.ts) also covers `saveBudget`, `setBudgetArchived`, and `deleteBudget`, and the time-off callables `saveLeaveType`, `setLeaveAllowance`, `addLeaveAdjustment`, `submitLeaveRequest`, `decideLeaveRequest`, and `cancelLeaveRequest`.
- [`functions/test/manual-time-entries.test.mjs`](../functions/test/manual-time-entries.test.mjs) covers `saveManualTimeEntry`.

Fixtures are written with rules disabled, the way trusted Admin SDK code would write them. When a rule changes, update this table and its test together.

For time-entry reads, verify that a member can directly get and query their own entries but cannot directly get another member's entry or run an unconstrained query over the collection. Owners and managers can directly get and query entries for every member; managers still cannot write another user's entries. Also verify boundary validation: unknown fields, oversized names/descriptions, invalid enum values, malformed timestamps, negative estimates, and completed entries with an end at or before the start must be denied. Direct client creates of completed entries and direct edits of completed entries must be denied; only starting and stopping a running timer, and changing a running timer's client, project, task, and billable state to active catalog items, is allowed from the browser, as is changing a running timer's description (which may be empty, up to 500 characters). Clients may record a non-empty `startTimeZone` of at most 100 characters when starting a timer, but may not set `autoStopped` or change either field afterwards. Verify the callable flows separately in the Functions emulator: `saveManualTimeEntry` rejects overlapping entries (including concurrent conflicting saves, of which only one may succeed) and entries that overlap a running timer, lets an edit overlap its own previous time, and lets only owners edit another member's entry, not managers, and clears `autoStopped` when an edit changes the end time (covered by `functions/test/manual-time-entries.test.mjs`); the scheduled auto-stop ends running timers at 24:00 of their start day in their start time zone (falling back to the member's time zone, then UTC), removes their `activeTimers` pointer, and leaves timers that were stopped meanwhile and timers whose day has not ended alone (covered by `functions/test/auto-stop-timers.test.mjs`); only an active workspace owner can create or revoke invitations, remove members, and change roles with `setWorkspaceMemberRole` (managers and members are refused, the owner's own role cannot change, only `manager` and `member` can be assigned, and removed members cannot be changed); invitation acceptance requires a matching verified email, pending status, and unexpired invitation; repeated acceptance is denied; the owner cannot remove themselves or another owner. The budget callables let active owners and managers create, edit, archive, restore, and delete any budget, including their own and the owner's, and refuse members; they reject an end before the start, impossible dates, ranges over 731 days, all-zero or out-of-range weekday minutes, unknown weekday keys, exception dates outside the range, unknown or archived projects and clients, and users who are not active members; they keep a budget's snapshots and allow edits after its member is removed or its target archived, but refuse to move a budget to a removed member or archived target; and they refuse to edit an archived budget or delete one that is not archived. The time-off callables let only the owner save leave types (including the built-in `holiday` and `sick` defaults, which have no document until saved) and refuse unknown types and invalid colors; let owners and managers set allowances and add noted adjustments for any active member, but refuse a manager's own allowance, members, half-day-invalid or out-of-range amounts, zero adjustments, and blank notes; count only working days, reject end-before-start, impossible dates, ranges crossing a year, ranges without a working day, half days on full-day types, archived or unknown types, and overlaps with the member's own pending or approved requests; mark requests over the remaining balance without blocking them and recompute that on approval; refuse anyone deciding their own request, members deciding at all, and deciding a request twice; auto-approve requests of types that need no approval and requests nobody else may approve (an owner without managers); and let the requester cancel pending requests and approved leave that has not started, and an approver cancel approved leave that has, while refusing other members. After removal, the former member must lose workspace reads and writes, including their budgets and time off, while the owner can still read the retained membership identity and historical time entries.
